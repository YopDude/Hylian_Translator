Zelda Hylian Translator

Welcome to the Zelda Hylian Translator! This translator supports English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, all major European languages (German, French, Spanish, etc.), and Slavic languages (Russian, Serbian, Ukrainian) by automatically converting them into the phonetic sounds used by Hylian scripts

Fonts for Japanese and English Input

b

Ocarina of Time and The Wind Waker feature Hylian scripts based entirely on Japanese

All other fonts in the collection, including those for Twilight Princess, Skyward Sword, and Breath of the Wild, are designed for use with the English alphabet.
